# Youvit image prompts (WhatsApp-ready, 15 Oct 2026)

**How to use**
- Copy one grey block, paste it into the Yobo WhatsApp chat as is. One image per message.
- The prompts use plain text only (no asterisks or underscores for formatting), so WhatsApp won't change them.
- Every prompt links the Drive folder and names two files in it (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1i48KxfIt-OR_7avIG8JI4zK4J6nTkY0Q):
  - the logo: `2023_YOUVIT_More_Life_Logo-01_2917x.webp`
  - the product photo: `youvit-<product>.jpg` (scraped from youvit.co.id, 15 Oct)
- **Check before using:** logo whole, right pouch, readable pouch text, no button, no extra claims, no face.
- **No Vitamin C+:** it isn't in the youvit.co.id online collection, so there's no product photo. The rainy-season immunity ad uses adult Multivitamin instead (Youvit's own listing says "untuk Imunitas").
