01 · The situation

Grüns opened ~6,300 doors in a year and passed a $300M annualized rate in month 24. Retail bought reach. It stopped leaving you a customer. And at $29.99 against $66.65 one-time, the discount only pays back if the household renews. So this plan does two things in 90 days: turn shelf buyers into subscribers, and keep the subscribers you have.

05 · The pack-to-profile loop
"Scan the pouch. Tell us your flavor. Your next pouch is on us." One QR turns a shelf unit into a named household.
1ScanQR on every pouch back and DTC shipper, plus a shelf wobbler where the retailer allows one. One line: "Scan for your free pouch."
2JoinTwenty seconds: email, phone, consent. Then three taps worth more than the contact. Which flavor? Low sugar or sugar-free? Kids at home? Plus the store, the only retail attribution you will ever own.
3Free pouchA single-use code, texted at once. Free pouch on the first subscription, or $20 off to buy once. The same $20 referral already pays, aimed at someone holding the product.
4Day-4 check-in"Four days in. How do they taste?" Two buttons: Love them / Not for me. Taste is why this brand works, so measure it per flavor.
5Review askLove them goes to Junip, then the listing they bought from. Not for me goes to a human and a flavor swap, never to a public listing.
Pouch-back QR panelFour SKUs plus seasonals · to be designed
Join pagegruns.co/join · to be designed

The reward and the review ask stay separate on purpose. Amazon and most retailer platforms ban incentivised reviews, and the FTC's 2024 rule carries real exposure. So the pouch buys the opt-in. The review ask goes out on day four with nothing attached.

Weeks 1-2Instrument & capture

Pull the Shopify export and build the real cohort curve: renewal by cohort, flavor, sugar level and adults-or-kids, with coded cancel reasons. Audit Klaviyo flows and consent. Connect Junip, Brand Analytics and the ad platforms. Design the QR and join page, and get the QR into artwork for the next run. Register the SMS sender. Agree the reward cost.

Gate: renewal curve in hand · cancel reasons coded · QR approved for the next run · baseline signed off
Weeks 3-6First campaigns

Pack-21 save goes live to the cohort nearest renewal, against a held-back control. One-time buyers get the conversion message. Day-4 check and split review routing switch on. Junip syndicates to Amazon and retailer listings. First household-stack test to subscribers with kids. QR joins start arriving.

Gate: renewal up vs control · join-to-subscribe at target · Amazon ratings holding · nothing sent unapproved
Weeks 7-13Automate & scale

The winners become the always-on journey. Insiders launches to the highest-renewal households and owns the holiday drop, so the seasonal opens to a list, not to paid traffic. Retailer landing pages make each chain's subscriber yield visible. A Monday scorecard covers renewal, joins per chain, and contribution per household by first channel. International doors get the same QR bridge on day one.

Gate: journey covers every active household · Insiders launch-week share measurable · renewal up vs Week 1 · international doors open with the loop running
