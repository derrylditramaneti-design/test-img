What does "860" mean?

  • It is a count of ads that are live right now, on each brand's official Meta advertiser page in the United States. That covers every format: video, image and carousel.
  • It covers every Meta placement: Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network. One ad can show in several of them.
  • It is not spend, reach or views. Meta doesn't publish spend for these ads, so the count shows how much each brand is testing, not how much it is paying.
  • One creative can count as several ads. Brands often run the same video with different copy or audiences, and each version is counted.
  • The "~" is Meta's rounding. The video number comes from Meta's video filter; the other column is the total minus video.

10 One move this week

Save the
second purchase.

Send a day-21 "box two" message before the $49.99 second purchase hits.

  1. Tell the subscriber it's coming: what renews, when, and for how much. That removes the surprise-charge complaint at the source.
  2. Make pausing or skipping one tap. It costs less than a cancellation and keeps the subscriber.
  3. Put the receipts next to the price: 60+ ingredients against AG1's 50, and the 12-week clinical result.
Why now

AG1 started its NSF ad 11 days ago and is putting $1.63 in front of the same buyer. Your second purchase price is the moment that comparison gets made.

Expected impact

We'll size it in week 1, once we can see your first-to-second order rate. We won't put a number on it until then.
