Structure, retargeting and budget

ComponentProposed treatment

06

The scorecard and next decision

MeasureDefinition / decision
Cost per paid customerRelevant media spend / deduplicated new paying customers in the agreed window.
Purchase conversionPaid outcomes / defined eligible visits, with a consistent denominator across treatments.
DiagnosticsClick cost, destination progression, lead quality if applicable and creative fatigue.
Pause / changeOperational or exposure limits trigger immediate review. Mature weak evidence triggers a specific message, audience or journey revision.
ExpandOnly when measurement, customer quality, economics and operating capacity support a defined next increment.

No forecast or profitability claim is made. Thresholds and minimum useful evidence must be set from the baseline before launch.
