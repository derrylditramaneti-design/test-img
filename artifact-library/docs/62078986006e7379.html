Acquire

New subscribers, bought when reach is cheap or intent is high, and pulled across from the shelf.

3 flightsPrime Big Deal Days, Black Friday to Cyber Monday, New Year's Day

Buy reach on the adherence claim when intent peaks, seed creators per moment, and bring shelf buyers home with a pouch QR.

Play 1Paid social

Paid social on the adherence claim

Format is no longer the difference. How often members take it is.

"Most supplements die in the cupboard. Grüns members take theirs most days."

How: lead with adherence, not format. Flight spend into Prime Big Deal Days, Black Friday to Cyber Monday and New Year's Day. Stay light through the World Series, where inventory is costly.

KPI

Cost per new subscriber, and the 60-day survival of each paid cohort

Run it yourself: 8h/wk, or Yobo Senior ($2,000/mo)

Play 2Creator / UGC

Creator volume, one brief per moment

Sub-1K creators built this brand. Give them a calendar, not a generic brief.

How: seed small creators every month with a brief keyed to the next moment: Child Health Day, Halloween, flu season, New Year.

A parent's 20-second Halloween video: one sachet in the lunchbox, the candy bowl left alone.

KPIPosts per brief within 21 days, and new subscribers per creator code

Run it yourself: 20h/wk, or Yobo Lead ($4,000/mo)

Play 3Retail / Amazon to on-site

The pouch QR bridge

Q4 shelf traffic is the biggest trial volume of the year. Give it a way home.

How: QR on every pouch back to a join page. Scan, pick a flavor, first subscription pouch on us.

"Scan the pouch. Tell us your flavor. Your next pouch is on us."

KPIJoins per 1,000 retail units, and join to subscription within 30 days

Run it yourself: 5h/wk, or Yobo Senior ($2,000/mo)

Retain

Retain means renewal, saves, cadence and win-back. Never a reorder play: the subscription already reorders.

Pack 21of 28: where the save lands

Save before the renewal with a flavor swap, a slower cadence or one skip, discount last. Time saves to the breaks, and win back by cancel reason.

  1. SMS and email

    The save before the renewal

    The save belongs before the renewal, not in the cancel flow.

    How: segment by packs remaining. At pack 21 of 28, offer a flavor swap, a slower cadence or one skip. Discount last.

    "A week from your next pouch. Try Berry this time, stretch to 6 weeks, or skip one?"

    KPIRenewal rate by cohort against a held-out control, and the skip-to-cancel ratio

    Run it yourself: 3h/wk, or Yobo Junior ($500/mo)

  2. SMS

    Calendar-aware cadence

    Breaks and travel are when a daily habit slips and a pile builds up.

    How: before Thanksgiving break and winter break, offer ship early or skip one. Use the end of Daylight Saving Time as a routine reset.

    "Travelling for Thanksgiving? Reply 1 to ship early, 2 to skip one."

    KPICancels in break weeks against the prior four weeks, and skip share

    Run it yourself: 2h/wk, or Yobo Advisor drafts it ($199/mo)

  3. Email

    Win-back by cancel reason

    A cancel reason is a brief. Answer the reason, not the person in general.

    How: households cancelled 60 to 240 days, split by coded reason. Price gets HSA/FSA via Truemed. Taste gets the new seasonal. Too much gets a 6-week cadence. Quitter's Day saves the January joiners.

    "You left because it piled up. Every 6 weeks works now."

    KPI60-day reactivation, and reactivated households surviving the next renewal

    Run it yourself: 2h/wk, or Yobo Junior ($500/mo)

The household stack

A subscriber with kids at home is the cheapest growth you have.

How: adults plus kids on one cadence and one shipment, offered to subscribers with kids at home. Halloween week is the hook.

"Your kids keep stealing them. Add a kids pouch to the same box."

The cultural calendar and the ship cutoffs set when a gift can still arrive.

"Ground Advantage cutoff is Dec 17. After that, an e-gift subscription in one tap."

KPIPrepay take-up, and gift subscriptions that reach a paid renewal
