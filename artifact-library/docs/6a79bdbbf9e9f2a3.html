01 · The situation

Grüns sits in about 6,300 doors and passed a $300M annualised rate in month 24. The subscription is $29.99 for 28 packs every 4 weeks, $1.07 a day, against $66.65 one-time. That discount only pays back if the household renews. About 10 seasonals land in 2026, and Junip holds 100,000+ reviews at 4.8.

Retail and Amazon reach is rented. The channel keeps the customer. Q4 is the loudest, most expensive quarter for reach, and the one where routines break.

Two risks shape every play. AG1 now sells gummies, so "powders taste like dirt" is fading; how often members actually take Grüns is the claim a competitor cannot copy. And discount dependency is already visible, so every Q4 offer here is shaped as access, cadence or a bundle, not a deeper discount.

05 · Retain

Retain means renewal, saves, cadence and win-back. Never a reorder play: the subscription already reorders.

1The save before the renewalSMS · Email
The save belongs before the renewal, not in the cancel flow.How: segment by packs remaining. At pack 21 of 28, offer a flavor swap, a slower cadence or one skip. Discount last."A week from your next pouch. Try Berry this time, stretch to 6 weeks, or skip one?"KPI: renewal rate by cohort vs a held-out control · skip-to-cancel ratioRun it yourself: 3h/wk, or Yobo Junior ($500/mo)
2Calendar-aware cadenceSMS
Breaks and travel are when a daily habit slips and a pile builds up.How: before Thanksgiving break and winter break, offer ship early or skip one. Use the end of Daylight Saving Time as a routine reset."Travelling for Thanksgiving? Reply 1 to ship early, 2 to skip one."KPI: cancels in break weeks vs the prior four weeks · skip shareRun it yourself: 2h/wk, or Yobo Advisor drafts it ($199/mo)

Weeks 1-4Instrument & test

Build the real renewal curve and code cancel reasons. QR into the next pouch artwork. A paid test on Prime Big Deal Days. The household-stack email in Halloween week. A routine-reset message as the clocks change. Pack-21 save goes live against a held-out control.

Gate: renewal curve in hand · save flow live vs control · creative approval under five business days
Weeks 5-9The BFCM push

Acquisition peaks on Black Friday to Cyber Monday with the adherence creative and creator briefs. Prepaid plans open on Cyber Monday. Diwali and Hanukkah gift subscriptions. Thanksgiving skip-or-ship-early prompts.

Gate: cost per new subscriber inside target · no rise in discount depth · break-week cancels below baseline
Weeks 10-13Protect & pre-load

Green Monday and the ship-cutoff gift push, then e-gift and shelf. Winter-break saves to every active household. Resolution creative and the January welcome built before New Year's Eve, so Jan 1 opens to a list, not only to paid traffic.

Gate: winter-break cancels below baseline · January creative approved · Monday scorecard by lever and channel
